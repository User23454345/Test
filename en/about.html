About us

Principled medical technology consulting

Medny is an independent consulting and procurement company for medical technology. We help healthcare facilities find the right devices, procure them cost-effectively and operate them safely.

Who we are

Medny was founded with a clear goal: decisions about medical technology should be based on facts – not on sales pitches. As an independent consultant and intermediary, we stand exclusively on the side of our clients.

Medny is run by an owner holding a Master's degree in Biomedical Engineering. This foundation in technology, medicine and regulatory affairs allows us not just to compare devices, but to truly understand them – and to explain the differences to you in plain language.

We are not a manufacturer and do not sell a product line of our own. That is precisely what makes our recommendations credible.

Important to know

Medny is neither a manufacturer nor a dealer with its own product range. We give impartial advice and broker the solution that fits your needs. Devices, service contracts and warranties are concluded directly between you and the respective supplier – we support you along the way.

What we stand for

Independence

We are not tied to any manufacturer and recommend only what is right for you, both professionally and economically.

Expertise

Biomedical engineering know-how meets experience in healthcare procurement processes.

Transparency

Comparisons you can follow, open communication and clear terms – with no small print.

Reliability

We keep our promises and remain your point of contact even after delivery.

Our services in detail

Needs analysis

It all starts with the question of what your facility actually needs. We record your workflows, rooms, interfaces and budgets and derive a robust requirements profile from them.

  • Recording clinical and organisational requirements
  • Assessment of rooms, infrastructure and IT interfaces
  • Preparation of a requirements profile as the basis for quotations

Independent consulting

We compare candidate devices and configuration options independently of any manufacturer and evaluate them by benefit, life-cycle costs, usability and service.

  • Market overview and objective device comparison
  • Assessment of ongoing costs: maintenance, consumables, training
  • Second opinion on quotations you have already received

Procurement & brokerage

We obtain comparable quotations, support negotiations and coordinate ordering, delivery, installation and acceptance.

  • Obtaining and comparing structured quotations
  • Support with negotiation and contract review
  • Coordination of delivery, installation and acceptance

Support & aftercare

It doesn't end with delivery: we support you with training and documentation, help with maintenance planning and remain your point of contact.

  • Organisation of user training and instruction
  • Support with the medical device logbook and operator obligations
  • Planning of maintenance as well as safety and metrological inspections

Our approach

Four steps, one point of contact. So you always have a clear overview – from the first conversation to ongoing operation.

  1. 1

    Needs analysis

    Together we clarify your needs: which examinations or treatments should be possible? Which rooms, utility connections and budgets are available?

    Outcome: Requirements profile with prioritised criteria

  2. 2

    Consulting

    We compare suitable devices and variants, explain advantages and disadvantages and give a well-founded recommendation.

    Outcome: Comparison overview and recommendation

  3. 3

    Procurement

    We obtain quotations, review terms, support the negotiation and coordinate delivery, installation and acceptance.

    Outcome: Quotation comparison, order and acceptance report

  4. 4

    Support

    We organise user training, support you with documentation and plan maintenance and inspections – so that your device runs safely and economically.

    Outcome: Training record, maintenance schedule, dedicated point of contact
