What we stand for

  • Vendor-neutral

    No product line of our own, no sales targets – only the solution that suits you.

  • Technical expertise

  • Transparent quotations

  • Support through to operation

    From the first analysis to commissioning and beyond.

Our services

We support you all the way from the first question to ongoing operation – or just where you need help.

Needs analysis

We capture your clinical, spatial and economic requirements and consolidate them into a clear requirements profile.

Independent consulting

We compare devices, manufacturers and configuration options objectively and explain the differences to you in plain terms.

Procurement & brokerage

We obtain quotations, review terms, support negotiations and coordinate delivery and installation.

Support & aftercare

We support you with training, documentation and maintenance planning and remain your point of contact whenever questions arise.

Our services in detail

How we work

A clear four-step process – so you know where your project stands.

  1. 1

    Needs analysis

    Together we clarify what your facility really needs – clinically, spatially and financially.

  2. 2

    Consulting

    We compare suitable solutions side by side and give a well-founded, impartial recommendation.

  3. 3

    Procurement

    We organise quotations, comparison, ordering and delivery – including installation and acceptance.

  4. 4

    Support

    Training, documentation and maintenance planning: we help you keep your device safely in use.
